About Me

Hello, my name is Mads Hoogeveen. I am 17 years old and I live in Amsterdam. I am currently studying Game Development at Media College Amsterdam. My hobbies are gaming, kung fu, tricking, basketball and sports in general. Since I was young I always enjoyed creating ideas and building things. Because I also loved gaming, game development felt like the perfect combination of creativity and technology. In the future I would like to start my own game studio where I can design and publish games together with a team.
