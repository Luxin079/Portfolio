About Me

Hello, my name is Mads Hoogeveen. I am 17 years old and I live in Amsterdam. I am currently studying Game Development at Media College Amsterdam. My hobbies are gaming, kung fu, tricking, basketball and sports in general. Since I was young I always enjoyed creating ideas and turning them into something playable.

Because I also loved gaming, game development felt like the perfect combination of creativity and technology. I enjoy experimenting with mechanics, building looping systems and improving the player experience through iteration. In the future I would like to work on my own games and help build a studio where ideas can become real projects.
